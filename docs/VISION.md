# Life Journal vision

Life Journal is a premium, private home for a person's memories. It is not a productivity system. Its value is emotional: preserving the places, people, details, and stories that would otherwise fade.

## Product promise

Life Journal should become more valuable with every year of a person's life. Opening it should feel rewarding even when there is nothing new to record.

## Current mission

Build an exceptional Travel Journal for the upcoming Spain trip. Planning supports the journey; memories are the product. Nutrition, fitness, shopping, projects, health, calendars, goals, habits, finance, relationships, and home are explicitly out of scope.

## Success looks like

- Capturing a moment takes less attention than the moment itself.
- A trip becomes a coherent, beautiful story rather than a folder of media.
- Private data is protected by default and portable by design.
- The finished journal and travel book are worth revisiting years later.
- New life modules can be added without weakening Travel Journal.

## Non-goals

No social feed, engagement loops, productivity scoring, enterprise dashboard, or feature-count competition.
